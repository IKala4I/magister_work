## 2. Assessment

- **Store name availability:** no app titled "Hourwell" exists on the App Store or Google
  Play as of 2026-08-31. The name is claimed at app-record creation (App Store Connect
  reserves the name; Play takes the title at listing) — first come, first served, so create
  the records early once the developer accounts exist (⛔ owner gates).
- **Confusion risk:** low. The closest planner-category name is "Hour: Daily Routine
  Planner" — a common-word title, not a house mark, and not "Hourwell". "HourWork",
  "Hourful", "Hourly" are different words in different categories.
- **Relevant Nice classes** if a registration is ever filed: 9 (downloadable software) and
  42 (SaaS). Registration is NOT required to publish on either store; both stores require
  only that the name not infringe an existing mark.
